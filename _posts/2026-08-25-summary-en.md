Firefox 157 will enable JPEG XL by default on all platforms, marking a significant milestone for the adoption of the next-generation image format. This move follows Chromium&\#x27;s similar decision, with both browsers now using the Rust-based jxl-rs library, while Apple has already shipped libjxl \(C++\) in its platforms. The change is expected to improve image compression and loading performance for web users, though it may require users on older operating systems like Windows 7/8 to upgrade to access the feature. The decision has sparked community discussion about cross-browser support and practical challenges with websites that do not yet accept JPEG XL uploads.

hackernews · yboris · Aug 25, 17:55 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49437946)

**「Background」** JPEG XL is a next-generation image format designed to offer better compression and features than legacy formats like JPEG and PNG, with support for high dynamic range and wide color gamut. It has been under development by the JPEG committee and has seen partial adoption in browsers; for example, Chromium had previously shipped support but later backtracked, while Firefox has been developing support behind a preference flag. The intent-to-ship announcement indicates that Firefox 157, due at the end of September, will enable JPEG XL decoding by default on all platforms, following its availability behind a Firefox Labs checkbox since version 152.

**「Impact」** Web developers and users will benefit from faster loading times and better compression for images, but they may encounter compatibility issues with websites and upload fields that do not yet support JPEG XL, potentially requiring manual conversion to JPEG or PNG.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters noted that both Firefox and Chromium are adopting the Rust-based jxl-rs library, raising questions about Apple&\#x27;s use of the C++ libjxl and potential performance comparisons. Some expressed a desire for browsers to automatically handle JPEG XL uploads on unsupported sites, while others wondered about support for older operating systems like Windows 7/8.

### [Chinese Team Sets World Record for Large-Area CZTSSe Solar Modules](https://www.ithome.com/0/994/266.htm) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A research team led by Meng Qingbo at the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, has fabricated large-area copper-zinc-tin-sulfur-selenium \(CZTSSe\) thin-film photovoltaic modules with a certified efficiency of 13.0%, setting a new world record for this type of module. The achievement was published in Nature Materials. The team introduced formamide to precisely regulate the coordination structure of metal ions and organic molecules, solving the problem of film uniformity and efficiency loss when scaling up device size. They developed a solution-coating process suitable for large-area continuous production, producing highly uniform CZTSSe films on 10 cm² substrates. Small-area cells \(1 cm²\) achieved a certified efficiency of 14.2%, while modules with six series-connected sub-cells \(total area 10.5 cm²\) maintained 13.0% efficiency. This work provides a new approach for low-cost, environmentally friendly thin-film photovoltaic manufacturing.

rss · IT HOME · Aug 25, 18:09

**「Background」** CZTSSe is a thin-film photovoltaic material composed of earth-abundant, low-cost, and environmentally friendly elements such as copper, zinc, and tin. It is considered a promising candidate for next-generation sustainable solar cells. However, scaling up the device size typically causes a sharp decline in film uniformity and efficiency, which has hindered its commercialization.

**「Impact」** This record-breaking efficiency for large-area CZTSSe modules demonstrates a viable path to scalable manufacturing of low-cost, green thin-film solar cells, potentially accelerating their commercial adoption and reducing reliance on scarce or toxic materials in photovoltaics.
